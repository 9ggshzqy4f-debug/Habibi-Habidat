# Project Brief — Habibi Habidat | Managed Housing as a Service

## Objective
Build a scalable managed-housing business that removes day-to-day STR/Mid-Stay operations from owners while improving revenue quality, guest experience and reporting. Frankel is the first design partner / managed portfolio.

## Core promise
Owner only: monthly payout/reporting and material decisions; operational work is run by Eishold.

## Competency pillars
1. Marketing & Advertising
2. Sales & Sales Channels
3. Support & Service
4. IT / Software / Automation / AI

## Commercial models
- Managed Core: €450/unit/month + 5% revenue + 15% revenue growth.
- Managed Growth: €650/unit/month + 7% revenue + 15% revenue growth.
- Owner Zero-Ops: €850/unit/month + 9% revenue + 20% NOI growth.
- Separate model: master lease / operator lease after legal and tax validation.

Minimum term 6 months, then monthly cancellable.

## Pilot pricing
Frankel launch partner: €450/unit/month + 5% Accommodation Revenue + 20% incremental contribution above an agreed 12-month baseline.

## Compliance boundary
The owner provides and warrants the object-specific legal basis/permissions for the intended letting model. Eishold documents the gate and does not silently guarantee legal eligibility. If evidence is missing, the unit does not go live.
